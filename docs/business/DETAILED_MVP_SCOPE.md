# BLONG MVP: Final Scope & Functional Specifications

## Project Vision
To launch the Minimum Viable Product (MVP) of the BLONG application, encompassing the complete user journey from being single to the wedding day (Phases 1, 2, and 3). The product will be a premium, trusted, and feature-rich platform tailored to the cultural nuances of Egypt and the Arab world.

---

## **Phase 1: Singles - The Serious Partner Search Experience**
**Goal:** To provide a secure, premium, and effective environment for users to find a serious, long-term partner, facilitated by a blend of AI and human curation.

### **1. Onboarding & Profile Creation**
* **Identity Verification & Seriousness Pledge**
    * **Purpose:** To ensure a safe community of genuine users from the very beginning.
    * **Functionality:** Upon registration, the user must agree to a "Seriousness Pledge". They are then required to upload a government-issued ID and a live selfie. An automated system verifies that the face matches the ID before the account is activated.
* **Interactive & Adaptive Compatibility Quiz**
    * **Purpose:** To gather deep, nuanced data about the user's personality, lifestyle, and values in an engaging way.
    * **Functionality:** The user engages with a conversational, dynamic quiz. Questions adapt based on previous answers (e.g., choosing "quiet weekend" leads to questions about hobbies, while choosing "social weekend" leads to questions about social preferences). It includes situational questions (e.g., "How would you handle this conflict scenario?") to assess behavior and emotional intelligence.
* **Visual "Style & Vibe" Profile**
    * **Purpose:** To capture the user's aesthetic and lifestyle preferences visually.
    * **Functionality:** The user selects from a curated set of images to create a mini-moodboard representing their preferred style for dates, partners, and life in general.
* **"Top 3 Non-Negotiables"**
    * **Purpose:** To give the user control over core deal-breakers.
    * **Functionality:** The user selects their top three most important values or traits from a predefined list. These act as hard filters for the matching algorithm.

### **2. The Matching Process**
* **AI & Human Concierge Matching**
    * **Purpose:** To combine technological scale with human wisdom for the highest quality matches.
    * **Functionality:** The AI engine analyzes the entire user base to find a shortlist of the most compatible partners based on all profile data. This shortlist is then reviewed by a human concierge who makes the final approval before presenting a match.
* **Weekly Concierge Progress Report**
    * **Purpose:** To keep waiting users engaged and informed.
    * **Functionality:** If no match is found, the user receives a weekly personalized update from their concierge, assuring them that the search is active and providing insight into the process.
* **Mutual Interest Card**
    * **Purpose:** To base the initial decision on true compatibility, not just photos.
    * **Functionality:** Before revealing full profiles, both parties receive a "card" showing only key points of compatibility (e.g., "You both share a passion for travel and value family"). Both must express interest to proceed.

### **3. The First Date Experience**
* **Pre-Date Briefing**
    * **Purpose:** To prepare users for a successful first date.
    * **Functionality:** After a date is confirmed and paid for, both users unlock a "Compatibility Report" with suggested conversation starters. The concierge also sends a helpful, personalized tip (e.g., outfit suggestion based on the venue).
* **"Date Companion" Tool**
    * **Purpose:** To provide an in-app tool to break the ice during the date if needed.
    * **Functionality:** An optional in-app feature with fun, lighthearted questions and simple games ("This or That?").
* **Post-Date Feedback Loop**
    * **Purpose:** To enable the AI to learn and improve.
    * **Functionality:** After the date, each user is prompted to provide simple, private feedback on the compatibility and chemistry. This data is used to continuously train the matching algorithm.

## **Phase 2: Engagement - The Collaborative Planning Journey**
**Goal:** To transform the often-stressful process of wedding and home planning into an organized, collaborative, and enjoyable journey that strengthens the couple's bond.

### **1. Core Planning Tools**
* **Wedding Checklist:** A manual, shareable to-do list where the couple can add, assign, and track all their wedding-related tasks.
* **Budget Tracker:** A collaborative tool to set a budget, manually log expenses by category, and add comments to discuss specific payments.
* **Shared Dream Board:** A Pinterest-style board for the couple to collect and comment on inspirational photos for their wedding vision.

### **2. Integrated & Advanced Planning**
* **Vendor Comparison Templates:** An interactive tool to enter quotes and details from multiple vendors into a side-by-side table for easy comparison.
* **Task Linking (Tasks, Vendors, & Expenses):** The core integration feature. A task on the checklist (e.g., "Pay photographer deposit") can be directly linked to the photographer's contact in the vendor list and the corresponding line item in the budget tracker.

### **3. Region-Specific Features**
* **Bride's Trousseau ("Gihaz") Manager:** A detailed checklist and budget tracker specifically for planning and purchasing all items for the new home.
* **Apartment Finishing Planner:** A project management tool to track the phases, vendors, and payments involved in finishing the marital apartment.
* **Digital "Qa'ima" Tool:** A tool to digitally log all items, with descriptions, values, and photos, and then export a professional, print-ready PDF for official signing.

---

## **Phase 3: Wedding Prep - The Final Countdown**
**Goal:** To provide powerful tools for managing the final, detail-intensive months and coordinating the wedding day itself.

### **1. Guest & Invitation Management**
* **Advanced Guest Management:** A full suite to import guest lists, collect addresses, track RSVPs in real-time, and design the seating chart using a drag-and-drop interface.
* **Digital Invitation Designer:** An in-app tool with templates to design, customize, and export both digital and print-ready wedding invitations.

### **2. Day-Of Coordination & Post-Wedding Wrap-up**
* **Wedding Day Timeline Manager:** A tool to create a minute-by-minute schedule for the wedding day that can be shared with vendors and the bridal party.
* **Guest Broadcast Tool:** A feature allowing the couple to send a push notification to all attending guests with last-minute updates on the wedding day.
* **Shared Guest Photo Album:** A private, shared album where all guests can upload the photos and videos they took during the wedding.
* **Thank You Card Assistant:** A post-wedding checklist generated from the guest list to help the couple track who they have sent thank-you cards to.
