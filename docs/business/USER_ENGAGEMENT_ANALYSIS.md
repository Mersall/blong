## 🎯 Engagement Strategy Overview

BLONG's user engagement strategy is built around **psychology-driven interactions**, **automated convenience**, and **premium experience delivery**. By eliminating dating fatigue through no-swipe matching and providing meaningful relationship progression tools, BLONG creates sustained engagement that leads to higher retention, premium conversion, and relationship success.

**Core Engagement Principles:**
1. **Quality Over Quantity**: Focus on meaningful interactions rather than endless browsing
2. **Psychology-First**: Every interaction reinforces psychological compatibility and growth
3. **Automated Intelligence**: Reduce user effort while maximizing relationship outcomes
4. **Cultural Sensitivity**: Respectful approach that honors different relationship values
5. **Lifecycle Support**: Engagement throughout the entire relationship journey

**Engagement Targets**: 85% 6-month retention, 50+ minute average sessions, 90%+ user satisfaction
